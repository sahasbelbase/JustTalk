First-Time Setup

First-Time Installation Guide

Just Talk is free, open-source software built independently without expensive corporate signing certificates ($99/yr Apple Developer / $400/yr Windows EV). Here is how to complete first-time setup safely in 10 seconds:

macOS (Gatekeeper Verification)

  1. Mount JustTalk-macOS.dmg and drag Just Talk to your /Applications folder.
  2. If macOS Gatekeeper shows "Apple cannot check it for malicious software":
    xattr -cr /Applications/JustTalk.app
    Run this one command in Terminal, or right-click JustTalk.app → click Open → click Open Anyway.
  3. Grant Microphone, Accessibility and Input Monitoring when prompted so Just Talk can hear you, react to Fn, and type into your apps.

Windows (SmartScreen Notice)

  1. Run JustTalk-Windows.exe installer.
  2. When Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC":
    Click "More info" → Click "Run anyway".
    This warning occurs for newly published open-source executables without commercial EV certificates.
  3. The installer runs in per-user space without requiring Administrator rights. Tap Right Alt to start voice typing.

Privacy & Security

Transparent data handling by design.

Audio recording

Your microphone is only recorded while dictation is active. Audio is kept in memory and cleared after transcription — it is never saved to disk. Whether audio is sent to a speech service depends on the engine you pick; Pure Offline Mode keeps it on your computer.

API keys & settings

API keys for AI formatting are stored only on your computer, in Just Talk's private app-data folder with owner-only permissions, and are sent only to the provider you selected. AI formatting sends text, never audio.

Pure Offline Mode

If you handle sensitive medical records, proprietary code, or confidential legal documents, toggle Pure Offline Mode. Just Talk bypasses every cloud service and types on-device transcriptions straight into your app.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Just Talk really free?

Yes. Just Talk is free and open source under the MIT license. There is no subscription, no account and no usage limit. If you turn on AI formatting with a paid provider, you pay that provider directly for the text it processes.

Does my voice recording leave my computer?

It depends on the speech engine you choose. With Pure Offline Mode, or the on-device Whisper engine, audio never leaves your computer. The built-in Apple engine uses on-device recognition when your Mac supports it for your language. The Google Web Speech engine — used for Nepali, and first on Windows when you are online — sends audio to Google to transcribe it. AI formatting only ever sends text, never audio.

How do I start dictating?

Tap Fn on macOS or Right Alt on Windows to start, speak, then tap again to type the text into the app you are using. Prefer holding the key while you talk? Turn on “Hold the shortcut while speaking” in Settings › Shortcut. Press Esc to cancel.

Can I dictate long messages with pauses?

Yes. Just Talk records for up to 5 minutes and keeps everything you said across pauses. In tap mode it stops on its own after 8 seconds of silence, so you can stop to think without losing your place.

What does AI formatting do to my text?

It adds punctuation, fixes grammar and spelling, removes filler words like “um” and “you know”, and resolves self-corrections. Say “can we meet at five, actually four” and Just Talk types “Can we meet at four?”. It never summarizes or changes what you meant, and you can turn it off with one toggle.

Which AI providers can I use?

Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, xAI Grok, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, any OpenAI-compatible API (for example NVIDIA), or Ollama running locally on your computer for completely private, free cleanup. Bring your own API key — it stays on your device.

Does Just Talk work offline?

Yes. Turn on Pure Offline Mode in Settings › Voice & Speech. Speech is transcribed by on-device models and nothing is sent over the network. Offline speech needs a one-time model download, sized to the languages you choose.

Does it support Nepali and Nepglish?

Yes. Speak Nepali, English or a mix of both. Just Talk can type Romanized Nepali (“k cha”), Devanagari (“के छ”), or translate to English — and in Automatic mode it picks the right one for each app, such as Romanized in chat apps and Devanagari in documents.

Which apps can I type into?

Any app with a text field: VS Code, Cursor, Slack, Chrome, Word, Notion, Gmail, WhatsApp, terminals and more. If no text field is focused, the text is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it yourself.

Can I speak one language and get English text?

Yes. Choose “Translate to English” on the Home screen. Whatever language you speak is typed as fluent English.

How does Action Mode work?

Press Shift while dictating to switch to Action Mode. Instead of typing your words verbatim, Just Talk follows them as an instruction — for example translating them into English.

Can I edit text I already wrote by voice?

Yes. Select the text in any app, hold your shortcut plus Shift (Fn + Shift on macOS, Right Alt + Shift on Windows) and say what to change, such as “make this shorter”, “make it more polite” or “translate to Nepali”. Just Talk rewrites the selection in place. This needs AI formatting turned on.

How do I paste my last dictation again?

Press Ctrl + Cmd + V on macOS or Win + Alt + V on Windows. Just Talk types your most recent dictation into the app you are using now — useful when the cursor was in the wrong place. You can also choose Copy Last Dictation from the tray or menu bar icon.

Why does macOS or Windows show an unidentified developer warning?

Just Talk is an independent open-source project and isn't signed with a paid Apple or Microsoft certificate. On macOS, run xattr -cr /Applications/JustTalk.app once in Terminal (or right-click the app and choose Open). On Windows, click “More info” then “Run anyway”. All source code is public on GitHub.

What are the system requirements?

macOS 12 or later on Apple Silicon or Intel, or Windows 10/11 (64-bit), plus a microphone. On-device models need 0.5–1.5 GB of disk space, depending on the languages you choose.

How do I update Just Talk?

Open Settings › Updates & Startup and click “Check for updates”. When a new version is available, Just Talk downloads and installs it in place and keeps all your settings, history and API keys.

Where are my API keys and history stored?

Only on your computer, in Just Talk's private app-data folder (~/Library/Application Support/JustTalk on macOS, %APPDATA%\JustTalk on Windows). Keys are saved with owner-only permissions and sent only to the provider you selected.

Can I download only the languages I speak?

Yes. Pick your languages during setup or in Settings › Languages & Models, and Just Talk downloads only the models those languages need — about 460 MB if you only speak English.

Can I use my own speech model or a local LLM?

Yes. Point Just Talk at any faster-whisper model on Hugging Face or a local folder for speech recognition, and use Ollama (for example Llama, Qwen or DeepSeek) for AI formatting with no internet and no token costs.

How is Just Talk different from paid voice typing apps?

It is free and open source, works on both macOS and Windows, lets you choose — or run locally — the AI that cleans up your text, has a fully offline mode, and has first-class Nepali and Nepglish support.
