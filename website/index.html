First-Time Setup

First-Time Installation Guide

Just Talk is free, open-source software built independently without expensive corporate signing certificates ($99/yr Apple Developer / $400/yr Windows EV). Here is how to complete first-time setup safely in 10 seconds:

macOS (Gatekeeper Verification)

  1. Mount JustTalk-macOS.dmg and drag Just Talk to your /Applications folder.
  2. If macOS Gatekeeper shows "Apple cannot check it for malicious software":
    xattr -cr /Applications/Just\ Talk.app
    Run this one command in Terminal, or right-click Just Talk.app → click Open → click Open Anyway.
  3. Grant Microphone and Accessibility permissions when prompted so Just Talk can listen while holding Fn and paste into your active apps.

Windows (SmartScreen Notice)

  1. Run JustTalk-Windows.exe installer.
  2. When Windows SmartScreen displays "Windows protected your PC":
    Click "More info" → Click "Run anyway".
    This warning occurs for newly published open-source executables without commercial EV certificates.
  3. The installer runs in per-user space without requiring Administrator rights. Hold Right Alt to begin voice typing!

Privacy & Security

Transparent data handling by design.

🎙️ Audio Recording

Your microphone is only recorded while your trigger key is physically held down. Audio is stored in volatile RAM, processed locally by faster-whisper, and immediately cleared. Audio files are never written to disk or sent across the internet.

🔑 API Keys & Local Config

If you choose to enable AI formatting, your Gemini API key is saved locally in your user folder at ~/.just_talk/config.json with restrictive file permissions. It is only sent directly to Google's official Gemini endpoint.

🛡️ Pure Offline Mode

If you handle sensitive medical records, proprietary code, or confidential legal documents, toggle Pure Offline Mode. Just Talk will bypass all cloud APIs entirely and paste raw Whisper transcriptions directly into your active cursor.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does my voice audio ever get uploaded to the cloud? ▾

No. Your microphone audio is processed 100% locally on your computer using faster-whisper and CTranslate2. In standard AI formatting mode, only the raw transcribed text (not your voice recording) is sent to Gemini for grammar cleanup. You can also turn on Pure Offline Mode in Settings for zero network requests.

Why does macOS or Windows show an untrusted developer warning? ▾

Just Talk is a community-driven open source project released under the MIT license. We do not charge subscriptions or purchase commercial signing certificates ($99/year Apple Developer, $400/year Windows EV). On macOS, run xattr -cr /Applications/Just\ Talk.app once. On Windows, click "More info" then "Run anyway". All source code is completely open for audit on GitHub.

How does Dynamic Action Mode work? ▾

Hold Fn on macOS or Right Alt on Windows to start dictation. If you press Shift while speaking, the floating HUD turns amber-gold into ⚡ Action Mode. When released, Just Talk instructs the AI to translate your speech into fluent English or execute commands instead of verbatim dictation.

Can I download only the language models I actually speak? ▾

Yes! During onboarding and in the Settings panel, you select your spoken languages. If you only select English, Just Talk downloads only the English model (~460 MB), saving over 1 GB of storage and memory.
